# AAA Open-World Techniques for a Fast, Dense, Destructible City

Research notes (October 2026). Each claim is tagged with how solid it is:
**[Official]** means a developer talk, blog or docs. **[Analysis]** means Digital Foundry or a frame dissection. **[Community]** means modder or forum reverse-engineering. **[Speculation]** is my inference or something unconfirmed.

---

## (a) Executive summary

Big open-world cities stay fast and look stable because **every building always has a representation on screen. Detail is swapped between versions; it is never torn down and rebuilt.** GTA V keeps a ladder of HD, LOD and SLOD models. Each level is a separate, pre-built asset that streams in and out, and the switch is hidden with a screen-door dither. Whole districts at the far end collapse into one mesh and one draw call. Insomniac's Manhattan streams fixed 128 m tiles, about one per second at top speed. Beyond those tiles it shows a persistent cache of 3D impostors and hand-built (later auto-generated) distant LODs. Window interiors are a shader trick (interior mapping), not geometry. The level of detail a building draws at is set by its distance and the time budget. Destruction is never the thing that moves it up or down. Destructible games keep destruction local: pre-fractured pieces swap in, dust masks the swap, physics runs only near the action, and the far city stays static and batched. For our Roblox Shibuya, two changes follow. First, keep a cheap, permanent facade proxy for every building and blend detail on top of it. Second, never delete and rebuild facades just because the camera moved.

## (b) Per-game findings

### GTA V (RAGE engine)

- **LOD ladder.** Map entities live in streamable `.ymap` files. Each HD entity has a parent LOD entity at the same position, and those link further up to SLOD (super-LOD) levels. Each entity's `LodDist` controls when its parent takes over. **[Community]** The levels are ILOD / LOD / SLOD (SLOD1-4 in modding tools) per [CFX forum: LOD and SLOD](https://forum.cfx.re/t/lod-and-slod/2550075). The parent/child setup (`ParentIndex`, `NumChildren`, `ChildLodDist`) is described in the [FiveM docs, Part 6: LODs](https://docs.fivem.net/docs/assets-manual/beginner-series/part-6/). Typical `LodDist` values are about 110-200 m for small props and 700-800 m for trees [community].
- **Districts merge into one mesh at a distance.** Vinewood Hills (several km², dozens of buildings) draws as **one 2,500-triangle mesh, one draw call, one diffuse texture**. Little Seoul's city blocks are likewise one low-poly mesh. Distant street lights are 32×32 quads batched into instanced geometry, and a distant car is just two headlight sprites. **[Analysis]** [Courrèges, GTA V Graphics Study pt. 2](https://www.adriancourreges.com/blog/2015/11/02/gta-v-graphics-study-part-2/). The same cheap world doubles as the source for real-time reflection cubemaps.
- **Hiding the pop.** LOD swaps use a **stipple/dither (screen-door) fade**, not an instant switch. **[Community]** Players see this as a dot pattern that ReShade cannot filter out. GTA IV's version was driven by a `stipple.wtd` texture ([ReShade forum](https://reshade.me/forum/shader-suggestions/2106-is-it-possible-to-filter-out-this-pattern-gta-v)). The exact blend timing is not documented. **[Speculation]**
- **Interiors.** Most buildings are shells with no interior. Enterable interiors are "MLOs": rooms linked by portals that render only when looked through, and stream separately from the shell. **[Community]** [FiveMX MLO guide](https://fivemx.com/mlo-vs-ymap/), [CFX portals thread](https://forum.cfx.re/t/solved-occlusion-portals-or-anything-else-outside-world-flickering/5283564).
- **Occlusion.** Hand-placed **box occluders** and occluder models are stored in ymaps. Big buildings hide everything behind them. **[Community]** [GTA V occluder tool](https://gta5mod.net/gta-5-mods/tools/3dsmax-gta-v-occluder-script-1-2v/), [MTA wiki](https://wiki.multitheftauto.com/wiki/SetOcclusionsEnabled).
- **Speed.** When flying, the game deliberately drops to lower LOD, and pop-in still happens. The PC version has a **"High Detail Streaming While Flying"** option, and the streamer can take about 5 s to catch up after a character switch. **[Analysis]** (Courrèges pt. 2.) This is the lesson: **cut detail by speed on purpose, so the streamer never falls behind.**

### GTA VI (release scheduled for Nov 19, 2026; not out yet)

- **Known:** Release is set for Nov 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/grand-theft-auto-6-delayed-again-but-itll-still-ship-in-2026)). Digital Foundry's analysis of the trailers and the Aug 2025 extended look praises **density across the whole scene**: crowds, traffic, vegetation, visible interiors and long sightlines all at once. It also notes extensive ray-traced lighting, a stable 30 fps at around 1440p on base PS5, and some fizzling and aliasing on fine detail. **[Analysis]** ([PureXbox on DF](https://www.purexbox.com/news/2025/05/gta-6-tech-is-seriously-impressive-but-its-looking-like-a-30fps-game-on-console), [Stevivor](https://stevivor.com/news/gta-6-s-extended-look-draws-comparisons-to-the-apollo/), [GTABoom](https://www.gtaboom.com/gta-6-at-30-fps-may-be-the-price-of-the-most-impressive-game-digital-foundry-has-ever-seen-785a)).
- **Speculation:** Rockstar has published nothing about streaming or LOD for GTA VI. It probably keeps the RAGE HD/LOD/SLOD hierarchy with SSD-era streaming. The 30 fps target is reportedly driven by CPU-heavy simulation of crowds and AI, not rendering. **Do not treat any GTA VI LOD details as fact.**

### Marvel's Spider-Man (2018) / Miles Morales / Spider-Man 2 (Insomniac)

- **Tiles.** About **800 tiles, each 128 m square.** The engine unloads behind the player and loads ahead, and at top swing speed it loads **"about one new tile every second."** **[Official]** [PlayStation Blog interview](https://blog.playstation.com/?p=204132). The world is a fixed grid with a fixed budget per tile.
- **Distant city = 3D impostors in a persistent cache.** The SIGGRAPH Asia 2018 brief "Spider-Man IG-Impostors: Cityscapes and Beyond" says flat billboard impostors could not reach the quality they needed. They built 3D impostors for the mid-to-far cityscape, held in a **persistent cache** reused across views, and **there are no 2D impostors in the game.** **[Official]** [SA2018 session](https://sa2018.siggraph.org/en/attendees/technical-briefs/session/184/details.htm).
- **Distant LODs.** Spider-Man (2018) and Miles Morales used a **manual distant-LOD pipeline**. Spider-Man 2's city is about twice the size, so they automated distant-LOD generation using mesh analysis. **[Official]** Abstract of GDC 2024, Kircher, ["Applied Mesh Analysis: Automating Distant-City LODs"](https://gdcvault.com/play/1034212/Applied-Mesh-Analysis-Automating-Distant) (listed as free on the Vault). Brooklyn and Queens were "low-poly representations" before Spider-Man 2 made them playable ([PS Blog, SM2](https://blog.playstation.com/?p=384475)).
- **Procedural markup.** Houdini placed building and ground dressing, traffic and crimes. Systems were designed so procedural output could still be hand-polished. **[Official]** [Santiago, GDC 2019 video](https://vimeo.com/326448655), [GDC Vault](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1025765). Ruskin's [Technical Postmortem (GDC 2019)](https://gdcvault.com/play/1026496) covers fitting NYC on a Blu-ray and the "perils of pre-baked lightmaps."
- **Window interiors.** Rooms behind windows are **interior mapping**: a shader ray-casts into a virtual room box (often a cubemap) inside a flat window quad. **No geometry exists behind the glass.** **[Analysis/Community]** Insomniac never officially confirmed the 2018 method ([Joost van Dongen on Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/programming/interior-mapping-rendering-real-rooms-without-geometry), [Zucconi](https://www.alanzucconi.com/?p=10105), [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/20231201-23558/)). For Spider-Man 2, an Insomniac tech-art portfolio describes a window shader with **parallax interiors from baked emissive cubemaps** and a separate **render-target interior** option ([J. Tuason, ArtStation](https://jtuason.artstation.com/projects/XgVxQL)). Windows now show occupants ([PS Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/?p=384475)). The trick holds up from mid-distance and breaks only very close.
- **Fast traversal.** Spider-Man 2 leans on the PS5 SSD for loading-free fast travel and faster swinging and gliding. Digital Foundry noted improved LOD and density. **[Analysis]** ([DTF translation of DF](https://dtf.ru/playstation/2199262-digital-foundry-tehnicheskii-obzor-marvels-spider-man-2)).

### Destruction games

- **Red Faction: Guerrilla (Geo-Mod 2.0).** It dropped the original game's real-time CSG for **pre-broken meshes** connected by a **stress model**. Remove supports and the structure collapses. **[Community/press]** [Red Faction Wiki](https://www.redfactionwiki.com/wiki/GeoMod_2.0), [GamesBeat review](https://gamesbeat.com/red-faction-guerrilla-review/). Note that they moved *away* from runtime CSG.
- **Battlefield (Frostbite).** Bad Company 1-2 used **destruction masking**: a damage mask is blended onto intact geometry so holes and cracks appear without new meshes. Frostbite 2 switched to volume distance fields (spheres) baked into low-res volume textures and drawn as **deferred decals** to speed up the workflow. **[Official]** [Kihl, SIGGRAPH 2010](https://www.slideshare.net/DICEStudio/siggraph10-arrdestruction-maskinginfrostbite2). Destructible pieces are authored as discrete parts with intact and destroyed states. **[Speculation]**: inferred from the per-part UV workflow the talk mentions.
- **The Finals (Embark).** Destruction is **server-authoritative** and identical for every player. Most of the environment can be destroyed, debris is physically simulated, and the ground is indestructible. **[Official]** [GDC 2024, Isaksson, "Engineering Mayhem"](https://gdcvault.com/play/1034280/Engineering-Mayhem-Technical-Deep-Dive), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2023/12/18/the-finals-embark-studios-road-map). Arenas are small and bounded, not a whole city.
- **Crackdown 3.** Full cloud (Azure, Cloudgine) destruction shipped **only in a bounded multiplayer mode**. The open-world campaign had limited destruction. **[Official/press]** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crackdown_3), [TheSixthAxis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2017/06/22/crackdown-3-will-only-feature-cloud-powered-destruction-in-multiplayer/). Even with the cloud behind it, a fully destructible open city was cut back.
- **Teardown.** Everything is voxels, and the large levels hold around half a billion of them. Destruction is uniform because there is no separate LOD mesh to keep in sync. Levels are small for this reason. **[Press]** ([PCGH](https://www.pcgameshardware.de/Teardown-Spiel-73677/Specials/PC-Game-Test-Benchmark-Voxel-Dynamic-Destruction-1394545/2/)).

### Engine-level references (HLOD, dithering, Roblox)

- **HLOD (Unreal).** Clusters of nearby static meshes merge into **one proxy mesh with one atlased material**, so many draws become one. Layer types are Instanced, Merged, Simplified and Approximated, and proxies stream on their own grid. **[Official]** [Epic HLOD overview](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/hierarchical-level-of-detail-overview-in-unreal-engine), [World Partition HLOD](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/world-partition---hierarchical-level-of-detail-in-unreal-engine).
- **Dithered LOD transition.** Both LODs render with complementary stipple masks during a short window, which costs a little GPU time and greatly reduces visible popping. **[Official]** [Cesium for Unreal](https://cesium.com/blog/2022/10/20/smoother-lod-transitions-in-cesium-for-unreal/).
- **Roblox (directly applicable).**
  - `Model.LevelOfDetail = StreamingMesh/SLIM` shows engine-built composite or impostor meshes for models that have streamed out. **Models that are modified at runtime are not supported.**
  - Keep models small and spatially grouped. Split large container models.
  - `Player:RequestStreamAroundAsync()` pre-loads a destination. `PredictiveStreamingMode = Enabled` pre-fetches along the player's velocity and heading.
  - **[Official]** [Streaming techniques](https://create.roblox.com/docs/workspace/streaming/techniques), [PredictiveStreamingMode](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/enums/PredictiveStreamingMode), [Instance streaming](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/workspace/streaming).

## (c) Principles

1. **Every building always has a representation, and detail swaps between versions.** GTA's HD/LOD/SLOD and Insomniac's persistent impostor cache never leave a hole. *For a fast destructible city:* at 600 studs/s, anything rebuilt on demand arrives late, so a permanent cheap proxy means there is never an empty frame or a visible build-up.
2. **Pre-build the levels and stream them; never generate them at runtime.** RAGE LODs and Insomniac distant LODs are offline assets, hand-made or auto-generated. *Why:* runtime part creation and CSG are what cause our hitches. Swapping between ready instances (parenting, or `LocalTransparencyModifier`/Transparency) is cheap by comparison.
3. **Merge far-away detail per block (HLOD).** Vinewood Hills becomes one mesh and one draw call. *Why:* 2,000 buildings × N facade parts is a draw-call and instance-count problem. Use one proxy mesh or union per block or tile, and keep the batch static.
4. **Use textures and shaders for windows, not parts.** Interior mapping, parallax cubemaps and emissive textures give apparent depth at zero geometry. *Why:* our pseudo-3D bay interiors should be a texture or decal trick on a single facade surface at mid and far range. Real geometry belongs only within close range of the action.
5. **Hide transitions: dither or fade, never snap.** GTA's stipple fade and Unreal's dithered LOD transition both work this way. *Why:* on Roblox, tween Transparency on overlapping LOD and HD representations for about 0.2-0.4 s, or swap under dust, motion blur or camera shake.
6. **Use hysteresis on LOD switch distances.** Bring detail in at distance D and drop it at D + margin. *Why:* a target or camera oscillating near a threshold otherwise flickers and repeatedly causes hitches. (Standard practice; **[Speculation]** about exact RAGE values.)
7. **Lower detail by speed on purpose.** GTA drops LOD while flying. *Why:* during a 600 studs/s launch, nobody can read facade detail anyway. Pin to the proxy level until the target slows down, which also frees budget for CSG.
8. **Predict along the velocity vector.** Insomniac loads tiles ahead of the player, and Roblox's predictive streaming uses velocity and heading. *Why:* we already compute the hit endpoint (`STRENGTH/DRAG`), so we know exactly where the target lands. Pre-warm detail around that point the moment the hit fires (`RequestStreamAroundAsync` or a client-side warm-up).
9. **Give each frame a fixed budget and spread work over time.** One tile per second is a fixed, predictable rate. *Why:* do at most N facade swaps or N CSG ops per frame, and queue the rest with nearest-first priority. Hitches come from bursts, not from the average load.
10. **Keep destruction local and pre-fractured; leave the far city static.** Red Faction moved from CSG to pre-broken meshes, and Crackdown and The Finals bound destruction to an area. *Why:* only the impact corridor needs live CSG. Everything else stays batched and stable.
11. **Mask swaps with VFX.** We already do this with `YIELD_MASK_DELAY` plus dust. DICE masks damage with decals instead of new geometry. *Why:* any intact-to-damaged swap, including a LOD swap near an impact, is invisible under a dust burst.
12. **Damage decals before geometry.** Frostbite's distance-field decals show cracks and scorch marks without remeshing. *Why:* far or minor hits can be a decal, saving CSG for close, large holes.
13. **Run physics only near the camera or target.** Debris far away anchors, sleeps or despawns, and ragdoll gives way to kinematic animation. *Why:* our debris and trench are physics; cap active bodies and freeze or remove them past a radius.
14. **Destroyed state must outrank LOD.** A building that has been hit must not reappear intact when its LOD changes. *Why:* Roblox SLIM does not support runtime-modified models. Once carved, a building should switch to "damaged" handling, or its proxy should be updated or hidden. **[Speculation]**: how AAA games sync this is rarely documented.

## (d) What NOT to do

- **Do not tear down facade detail and rebuild it when the camera moves.** No AAA game we found does this. Swap between existing representations, or fade them.
- **Do not create Instances or run CSG during traversal unless you have a per-frame budget.** Creating parts during a launch is a hitch generator.
- **Do not leave a gap between "detail removed" and "proxy shown."** The proxy must be visible *before* the detail goes away, which is why the fades overlap.
- **Do not switch on a single hard distance threshold with no hysteresis.** That causes flicker and repeated hitches.
- **Do not model window interiors as real geometry at range.** Use textures or shaders, and add geometry only near the action.
- **Do not let LOD resurrect destroyed geometry, or show intact HD over a carved proxy.**
- **Do not apply full physics to distant debris,** and do not leave unbounded debris counts awake.
- **Do not make the whole city one giant container model,** because it defeats Roblox streaming and LOD. Do not mark everything Persistent either.
- **Do not chase full-city real-time destruction.** Even Crackdown 3 with the cloud limited it. Bound it to the hit corridor.
- **Do not treat GTA VI tech claims as fact.** Nothing about its LOD or streaming has been published.
